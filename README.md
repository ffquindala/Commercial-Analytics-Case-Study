# Commercial Analytics Case Study – Customer Acquisition & Profitability Analysis

This project demonstrates an end-to-end commercial analytics solution developed using SQL and Power BI. 
The objective was to evaluate the profitability of customer acquisition channels by combining customer acquisition data, payment activity, and pricing rules to generate business insights and strategic recommendations.

# Business Objectives

The analysis aimed to answer key business questions such as:
Which acquisition channel generates the highest profit?
What is the return on investment (ROI) of each acquisition channel?
Which payment methods contribute the most to revenue and profitability?
Which pricing models perform best?
How well are customers retained after acquisition?
What strategic actions should the business take to improve profitability?

# Solution Approach
1. Data Understanding
Three datasets were analyzed:
Customer Acquisition:

Payments:
Contains monthly payment activity including:
Payment method
Number of transactions
Payment volume

Pricing:
Contains pricing rules for each payment method including
Fixed transaction fee

# Business Questions & Analytical Findings

The project was designed to answer key commercial questions by combining customer acquisition data, transaction data, and pricing information. 
The objective was not only to build dashboards, but to provide actionable insights that could support strategic business decisions.

1. Which acquisition channel is the most profitable?
Analysis

Compared revenue, acquisition cost, profit, and ROI across customer acquisition channels.

Business Insight

Identified the channels that generated the highest return after accounting for acquisition costs.

Recommendation

Increase investment in high-performing acquisition channels while reviewing the performance of lower-performing ones.

2. Are acquisition costs being recovered?
Analysis

Compared total revenue generated by customers acquired through each channel against the corresponding acquisition investment.

# Business Insight and Recommendation

Determined whether customer acquisition activities generated sufficient value to justify marketing spend.

Recommendation

Continue investing in profitable channels and reassess channels with low or negative returns.

3. Which payment methods generate the highest revenue?
Analysis

Calculated revenue for each payment method using fixed and variable pricing rules.

# Business Insight

Identified the payment methods contributing most to overall revenue.

Recommendation

Promote payment methods with stronger commercial performance and investigate opportunities to increase their adoption.

4. Which payment methods are the least profitable?
Analysis

Measured profit generated by each payment method after considering pricing structure.

Business Insight

Identified products or services that contribute little value or reduce overall profitability.

Recommendation

Review pricing strategies, customer adoption, or operational costs associated with low-performing payment methods.

5. Which pricing model performs best?
Analysis

Grouped payment methods according to their pricing structure (e.g., fixed fee, hybrid pricing) and compared revenue and profitability.

Business Insight

Determined which pricing strategy generated the strongest commercial performance.

Recommendation

Evaluate whether successful pricing models can be expanded to other products or services.

6. How is customer revenue distributed across acquisition channels?
Analysis

Compared customer count, transaction volume, and generated revenue by acquisition channel.

Business Insight

Distinguished between channels acquiring many customers and those acquiring higher-value customers.

Recommendation

Prioritize channels that attract customers with higher long-term value rather than focusing solely on acquisition volume.

7. How well are customers retained over time?
Analysis

Performed cohort analysis using customers' first transaction dates to measure retention over subsequent months.

Business Insight

Measured customer loyalty and identified trends in customer engagement after acquisition.

Recommendation

Develop retention initiatives for cohorts showing early signs of customer churn and replicate successful acquisition strategies across channels.

8. What is the return on investment (ROI) of each acquisition channel?
Analysis

Calculated ROI by comparing channel profit with acquisition investment.

Business Insight

Measured the efficiency of marketing and acquisition spending.

Recommendation

Allocate future acquisition budgets based on ROI rather than customer volume alone.

9. Which customers generate the greatest commercial value?
Analysis

Evaluated revenue and transaction activity generated by customers acquired through different channels.

Business Insight

Identified customer segments that contribute the highest business value.

Recommendation

Focus acquisition efforts on customer profiles associated with higher lifetime value.

10. What strategic actions could improve profitability?
Analysis

Combined insights from revenue, profitability, pricing models, acquisition channels, and customer retention.

Business Insight

Identified the primary drivers of business performance and areas for optimization.

# Recommendations 

Increase investment in high-performing acquisition channels.
Review underperforming payment methods and pricing strategies.
Improve customer retention initiatives.
Monitor profitability using channel-level KPIs and ROI.
Continuously optimize commercial strategy based on data-driven insights.

# Business Value Delivered

This analysis transformed transactional and customer data into actionable commercial insights by identifying profitability drivers, evaluating acquisition efficiency, measuring customer retention, and supporting strategic decisions related to marketing investment, pricing, and revenue optimization.
