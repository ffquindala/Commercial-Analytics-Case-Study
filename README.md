# Commercial Analytics Case Study – Customer Acquisition & Profitability Analysis

This project demonstrates an end-to-end commercial analytics solution developed using SQL and Power BI. 
The objective was to evaluate the profitability of customer acquisition channels by combining customer acquisition data, payment activity, and pricing rules to generate business insights and strategic recommendations.

# Business Objectives

The analysis aimed to answer key business questions such as:
Which acquisition channel generates the highest profit?
What is the return on investment (ROI) of each acquisition channel?
Which payment methods contribute the most to revenue and profitability?
Which pricing models perform best?
How well are customers retained after acquisition?
What strategic actions should the business take to improve profitability?

# Solution Approach
1. Data Understanding
Three datasets were analyzed:
Customer Acquisition:
Contains customer information, acquisition date and acquisition channel.

Payments:
Contains monthly payment activity including:
Payment method
Number of transactions
Payment volume

Pricing:
Contains pricing rules for each payment method including
Fixed transaction fee
Variable fee based on payment volume

# Revenue Calculation

Revenue was calculated for every payment record using the pricing rules.

Revenue consisted of:

Fixed fee × Number of Transactions
Variable fee × Payment Volume

This created a dynamic revenue model that adapts automatically to each payment method.

4. Cost & Profitability Analysis

A commercial profitability model was built by combining:

Revenue
Acquisition costs
Customer acquisition channels

Key business metrics included:

Revenue
Profit
ROI
Revenue per customer
Revenue per transaction
5. Customer Analysis

Customer behaviour was analysed to understand:

Number of acquired customers
Revenue generated by each channel
Customer contribution to overall profit
6. Pricing Model Analysis

Payment methods were grouped according to their pricing structure:

Standard Fixed Fee
Premium Fixed Fee
Fixed + Variable Fee

This allowed comparison of profitability across different pricing models rather than individual payment methods.

7. Cohort & Retention Analysis

Customer cohorts were created using the first payment date.

Retention analysis measured customer activity over consecutive months after acquisition, providing insights into customer lifetime behaviour.
